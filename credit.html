    How a Fiado Works

    From the purchase to the final payment, everything in order.

    The client brings their products

    You select the products and add them to their account.

    They agree on installments and dates

    You define how many payments will be and how often they will be paid.

    Reminder before each payment

    The system notifies you so you can collect on time without causing inconvenience.

    You register the payment

    Each payment reduces the balance and is saved in their history.

    Account settled, higher limit

    When completed on time, the client moves up to a higher level of trust.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do I have to charge interest?

    It's not mandatory. The surcharge is optional and you decide for each fiado; you can leave it at 0%.

    What happens if a client is late?

    Their account appears marked as "In default" and you can send them a reminder. As long as they are not up to date, they don't advance in level.

    Can I change a client's limit?

    Yes. The levels are a guide and you decide the limit you give to each person.

    Do clients see their account?

    They can check their balance, installments, and payment dates to avoid confusion.
